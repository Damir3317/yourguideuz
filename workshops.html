Learn From Local Artisans

Hands-on masterclasses with Uzbekistan's craftspeople — cook, weave, paint and shape something to take home.

Hands-On Experiences

Masterclasses & Workshops

Plov Cooking Masterclass

Cook Uzbekistan's national dish from scratch with a local family, then sit down together to enjoy what you made.

Suzani Embroidery

Learn traditional silk embroidery stitches from Bukhara artisans, and stitch your own small keepsake.

Silk Paper Making

In the paper village of Konigil, press your own sheet of mulberry-bark paper using centuries-old techniques.

Ceramics & Pottery

Shape and glaze your own piece of pottery in the Rishtan or Gijduvan style, guided by a master potter.

Miniature Painting

Try your hand at the fine brushwork of Timurid-era miniature art, under the eye of a local painter.

Tandoor Bread Baking

Shape and bake traditional non bread in a clay tandoor oven, then take your own loaf home warm.
